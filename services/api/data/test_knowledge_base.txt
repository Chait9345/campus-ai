=== COMPANY RECRUITMENT PROCESS ===

Question: What is the typical recruitment process at our campus?
Answer: The typical recruitment process includes the following stages:
1. Pre-placement talks (PPT) - Company introduces itself and job roles
2. Online assessment - Coding test, aptitude test, or case study (2-3 hours)
3. Technical interview - 45-60 minutes covering data structures, algorithms, and system design
4. HR interview - 20-30 minutes discussing background, motivation, and cultural fit
5. Offer letter - Issued within 1-2 weeks after final round

=== INTERVIEW PREPARATION ===

Question: How should I prepare for technical interviews?
Answer: Here's a comprehensive preparation guide:
1. Data Structures & Algorithms (3-4 weeks):
   - Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs, Hash Tables
   - Sorting, Searching, Dynamic Programming
   - Practice on LeetCode, HackerRank (minimum 100 problems)

2. System Design (2-3 weeks):
   - Database design, scalability, caching
   - Microservices, API design
   - Study: Designing Data-Intensive Applications

3. Core Subjects (2 weeks):
   - Operating Systems, DBMS, Computer Networks
   - Review class notes and standard textbooks

4. Mock Interviews (2 weeks):
   - Practice with peers or use platforms like Pramp
   - Record yourself and review

5. Company-specific prep (1 week):
   - Research company tech stack
   - Review recent projects and blog posts

=== PLACEMENT RULES AND REGULATIONS ===

Question: What are the placement rules and regulations?
Answer: Key placement rules:
1. Students must maintain minimum attendance (75%)
2. No active backlogs at the time of placement
3. Students can accept maximum 2 offers (one primary, one backup)
4. Offer acceptance is binding - cannot reject after accepting
5. Joining date cannot be postponed beyond 6 months
6. Students must inform placement office of any changes
7. Ethical conduct during interviews is mandatory
8. No plagiarism or dishonesty in applications

Violations may result in debarment from future placements.
